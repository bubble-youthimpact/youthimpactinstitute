<h3>Executive Summary</h3>
<p>Youth entrepreneurs are increasingly driving green innovation and social impact ventures that address climate change and social inequities. Federal support tailored to these sectors remains limited, reducing the potential for scalable environmental entrepreneurship. This memo recommends the EPA expand targeted grant programs, mentorship, and capacity-building initiatives for youth-led green and social enterprises. Such investments align with federal climate goals and youth demands for sustainability and justice.</p>
<h3>Background</h3>
<ul><li>Climate change and social inequity disproportionately affect youth and marginalized communities.</li><li>Green entrepreneurship provides pathways for economic opportunity and environmental stewardship.</li><li>Federal programs often overlook youth-led ventures or require traditional business models incompatible with social enterprises.</li><li>Existing green grants focus primarily on established businesses, limiting youth innovation.</li></ul>
<h3>Analysis</h3>
<p>Targeted support can:</p>
<ul><li>Accelerate youth-driven solutions addressing local environmental challenges.</li><li>Foster community resilience through sustainable business models.</li><li>Enhance youth engagement in climate policy and economic inclusion.</li><li>Without tailored funding and mentorship, youth may face barriers scaling green ventures.</li></ul>
<h3>Recommendations</h3>
<p>EPA should:<br>Create youth-focused green entrepreneurship grant programs with flexible criteria.</p>
<ul><li>Partner with environmental nonprofits and youth organizations for outreach and technical assistance.</li><li>Support mentorship linking youth to experienced green business leaders.</li><li>Incorporate impact measurement frameworks to track social and environmental outcomes.</li><li>Promote public awareness campaigns highlighting youth-led environmental innovation.</li></ul>
<h3>Conclusion</h3>
<p>Supporting youth green entrepreneurship is critical for addressing climate change and advancing environmental justice. The EPA’s targeted federal support will empower youth innovators and strengthen sustainable economic development nationwide.</p>
