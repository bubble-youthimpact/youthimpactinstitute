<h3>Executive Summary</h3>
<p>In regions where climate-induced migration meets border militarization, young migrants are increasingly treated as security threats. This memo urges the IOM to intervene against the growing criminalization of youth in climate migration corridors, and to promote humanitarian border governance rooted in dignity and international law.</p>
<h3>Background</h3>
<p>Climate change is reshaping migration routes and enforcement:</p>
<ul><li>Youth climate migrants are crossing increasingly securitized borders in Central America, West Africa, and the Sahel.</li><li>Armed patrols, detention centers, and surveillance technologies disproportionately target youth migrants.</li><li>Criminalization undermines humanitarian protection and increases migrant deaths.</li></ul>
<h3>Analysis</h3>
<p>Treating climate-displaced youth as criminals is a human rights crisis:</p>
<ul><li><strong>Militarized Borders:</strong> Young migrants face arrests, tear gas, and pushbacks rather than assistance.</li><li><strong>Detention as Deterrent:</strong> Youth are detained in overcrowded, abusive facilities under anti-trafficking or smuggling laws.</li><li><strong>Climate Injustice:</strong> Migrants fleeing climate disaster are punished for consequences they didn’t cause.</li><li><strong>Global Hypocrisy:</strong> Wealthy nations investing in border tech are also responsible for historic emissions.</li></ul>
<h3>Recommendations</h3>
<h3>Humanitarian Border Governance</h3>
<ul><li>Promote IOM standards for climate-migrant reception that prohibit youth detention and collective expulsion.</li><li>Create safe mobility corridors for climate-affected youth across regions.</li></ul>
<h3>Decriminalization and Legal Protection</h3>
<ul><li>Oppose legislation that treats climate migration as smuggling or national security risk.</li><li>Fund legal aid and emergency shelters near border hotspots.</li></ul>
<h3>Accountability for Border Violence</h3>
<ul><li>Monitor and report on youth rights violations in climate migration zones.</li><li>Hold private security and border tech firms accountable for abusive practices.</li></ul>
<h3>Conclusion</h3>
<p>Youth should not be treated as criminals for fleeing climate collapse. The IOM must act to build migration systems that protect, not punish, those displaced by global injustice.</p>
