<h3>Executive Summary</h3>
<p>Faith-based climate action is on the rise—especially among young Christians who view environmental degradation as a moral failing. Yet the Republican Party often sidelines ecological messages in favor of culture war rhetoric. This memo urges the RNC to elevate faith-rooted environmentalism through partnerships with churches, youth ministries, and religious colleges. Doing so aligns with biblical stewardship, grows the conservative youth base, and expands moral credibility on climate.</p>
<h3>Background</h3>
<ul><li>65% of young white evangelicals now support some form of climate action.</li><li>Pope Francis and major Protestant groups have issued climate encyclicals or resolutions.</li><li>Faith-based disaster relief networks are often more effective than state responses.</li><li>Young Christian conservatives want purpose-driven engagement, not just partisan talking points.</li><li>Creation care movements are expanding in red states but lack party support.</li></ul>
<h3>Analysis</h3>
<ul><li>Neglecting religious environmentalism leaves moral leadership to Democrats.</li><li>Youth in faith communities are eager to connect service with sustainability.</li><li>Climate messaging framed in faith language resonates more than technocratic appeals.</li><li>There is political space to redefine “pro-life” to include environmental defense.</li></ul>
<h3>Recommendations</h3>
<h3>Faith and Climate Youth Fellowship</h3>
<ul><li>Support a national program for young conservatives to lead sustainability projects through religious organizations.</li></ul>
<h3>Creation Care Curriculum</h3>
<ul><li>Partner with seminaries and Christian colleges to create climate modules rooted in scripture and stewardship.</li></ul>
<h3>Church Infrastructure Grants</h3>
<ul><li>Fund energy retrofits for churches in disaster-prone areas, with youth-led design teams.</li></ul>
<h3>Faith-Led Policy Forums</h3>
<ul><li>Host dialogues between Republican lawmakers and religious youth on shared ecological concerns.</li></ul>
<h3>Conclusion</h3>
<p>Faith and environmentalism are not opposites—they are allies in the fight for a livable planet. By embracing youth-led creation care, Republicans can reclaim their moral voice and reconnect with a generation hungry for meaningful service and climate solutions</p>
