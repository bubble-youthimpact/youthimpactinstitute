<h3>Executive Summary</h3>
<p>Sexual violence at migration borders is widespread and underreported, especially among unaccompanied girls and LGBTQ+ youth. This memo urges UNHRC to address this crisis through independent investigations, survivor-centered policies, and a new legal standard for protection against border-based gender violence.</p>
<h3>Background</h3>
<p>Gendered violence along migration routes includes:</p>
<ul><li>Over 60% of girls report sexual violence during transit across the Americas (Doctors Without Borders, 2022).</li><li>LGBTQ+ youth face rape, forced labor, and "corrective" abuse in camps and detention centers.</li><li>Protection systems are gender-blind and often retraumatize survivors during documentation.</li><li>Border agents and smugglers act with impunity in many regions.</li></ul>
<h3>Analysis</h3>
<p>Borders are zones of normalized abuse:</p>
<ul><li><strong>Impunity and Secrecy:</strong> Lack of oversight enables abuse by state and non-state actors.</li><li><strong>No LGBTQ+ Protections:</strong> Gender identity and sexual orientation are not consistently grounds for asylum.</li><li><strong>Fear of Reprisal:</strong> Youth fear deportation or retaliation for reporting abuse.</li><li><strong>Silenced Testimonies:</strong> Legal systems do not accommodate trauma-informed statements or nonbinary identities.</li></ul>
<h3>Recommendations</h3>
<h3>Independent Monitoring and Reporting</h3>
<ul><li>Establish UNHRC special rapporteurs for gender-based violence in migration zones.</li><li>Fund cross-border survivor support centers with anonymous reporting tools.</li></ul>
<h3>Protection Framework Expansion</h3>
<ul><li>Advocate for the recognition of sexual orientation and gender-based violence as valid grounds for asylum.</li><li>Require anti-violence training and accountability systems for border officials and NGO partners.</li></ul>
<h3>Youth Survivor Advocacy</h3>
<ul><li>Fund survivor-led policy councils and arts-based healing initiatives.</li><li>Create secure online platforms for youth to share experiences and access legal aid.</li></ul>
<h3>Conclusion</h3>
<p>Sexual violence at borders is a political weapon against gendered bodies. The UNHRC must reimagine border protection not through militarization, but through gender justice and survivor power.</p>
