<h3>Executive Summary</h3>
<p>Globally, millions of children and youth on the move are denied consistent education, exacerbating cycles of exclusion and poverty. This memo urges the IOM to coordinate a transnational strategy ensuring educational access for migrant youth in transit zones, border encampments, and detention centers. A rights-based approach demands portable accreditation, multilingual curriculum materials, and safe, mobile learning spaces.</p>
<h3>Background</h3>
<p>Disruption to education during migration includes:</p>
<ul><li>Over 30 million displaced or migrant children are out of school globally (UNICEF, 2023).</li><li>Education is not prioritized in emergency or transit contexts.</li><li>National education systems rarely accept foreign-acquired credentials.</li><li>Hostile migration policies criminalize or detain youth, barring them from formal schooling.</li></ul>
<h3>Analysis</h3>
<p>Migration journeys fracture learning:</p>
<ul><li><strong>No Safe Spaces to Learn:</strong> Camps, shelters, and informal crossings lack consistent educational infrastructure.</li><li><strong>Documentation Barriers:</strong> Youth without legal IDs cannot enroll in school or sit for exams.</li><li>Language and Cultural Gaps: Curricula rarely address linguistic needs or migrant histories.</li><li><strong>Trauma and Instability:</strong> Constant movement and fear inhibit concentration and school attendance.</li></ul>
<h3>Recommendations</h3>
<h3>Portable and Flexible Education Systems</h3>
<ul><li>Develop an IOM-led transnational accreditation framework for mobile learners.</li><li>Support mobile classrooms and digital learning units in migration corridors.</li></ul>
<h3>Migrant Youth Pedagogies</h3>
<ul><li>Fund trauma-informed, multilingual education materials tailored to displaced youth.</li><li>Train educators from migrant communities to serve as peer learning facilitators.</li></ul>
<h3>Legal Protections and Policy Advocacy</h3>
<ul><li>Lobby governments to allow educational enrollment regardless of migration status.</li><li>Create legal safe zones for schools and learning spaces along migration routes.</li></ul>
<h3>Conclusion</h3>
<p>Denying education to youth in transit is a violation of their right to develop and participate in society. The IOM must lead a coordinated, cross-border response that treats education not as a luxury, but as a portable human right.</p>
