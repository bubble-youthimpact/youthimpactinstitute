<h3>Executive Summary</h3>
<p>Wildfires now threaten conservative strongholds across the American West. Rural youth are increasingly frustrated by the failure of partisan inaction on land management, insurance access, and fire prevention. This memo outlines a path for the RNC to lead on wildfire resilience through youth service programs, property-based forest stewardship, and disaster-readiness education. These solutions strengthen conservative values—responsibility, self-reliance, and intergenerational land protection—while addressing climate realities.</p>
<h3>Background</h3>
<ul><li>Wildfires burned 10 million acres in 2023, disproportionately affecting Republican-voting counties.</li><li>Over 70% of U.S. forest land is privately owned or managed.</li><li>Youth in fire-prone areas are forming mutual aid brigades and volunteering for fire prevention projects.</li><li>State firefighting budgets are overwhelmed, with insufficient federal coordination.</li><li>Conservative ranchers and landowners want less bureaucracy in forest management programs.</li></ul>
<h3>Analysis</h3>
<ul><li>Wildfires increasingly affect voter security, homeownership, and mental health in red states.</li><li>Embracing youth in land stewardship restores conservative credibility on environmental care.</li><li>There is electoral risk in denying disaster severity—particularly among young, pragmatic conservatives.</li><li>Market-aligned forest resilience can strengthen private property rights while reducing risk.</li></ul>
<h3>Recommendations</h3>
<h3>Youth Wildfire Corps</h3>
<ul><li>Create a volunteer and paid national corps to clear underbrush, maintain fire lines, and educate local communities.</li></ul>
<h3>Incentivized Forest Management</h3>
<ul><li>Expand tax credits and grants for private landowners who adopt thinning and controlled-burn protocols.</li></ul>
<h3>Fire-Ready Schools and Homes</h3>
<ul><li>Fund defensible space retrofits for low-income rural families and schools in wildfire zones.</li></ul>
<h3>Youth-Led Risk Communication</h3>
<ul><li>Train conservative student groups to lead emergency prep workshops in schools and churches.</li></ul>
<h3>Conclusion</h3>
<p>Republicans can lead the next generation of environmental responsibility—not by denying climate change, but by defending land and liberty through youth-led wildfire resilience. This approach protects rural traditions while facing tomorrow’s risks head-on.</p>
