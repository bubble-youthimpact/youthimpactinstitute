<h3>Executive Summary</h3>
<p>Decarbonizing transportation is central to climate mitigation—and young people are leading the shift away from car dependency. Yet federal transit funding lags behind highway expansion, and urban youth remain underserved by safe, reliable public transportation. This memo urges the DNC to embed youth-designed transit priorities into its climate platform, positioning mobility justice as a linchpin of environmental equity and generational trust.</p>
<h3>Background</h3>
<ul><li>Transportation accounts for 29% of U.S. greenhouse gas emissions.</li><li>Gen Z drives less and supports public transit more than any previous generation.</li><li>Black, Brown, and low-income youth face the longest, most polluting commutes.</li><li>Transit deserts isolate youth from job, school, and civic opportunities.</li><li>Youth-led climate coalitions consistently rank transit access as a top justice demand.</li></ul>
<h3>Analysis</h3>
<ul><li>Young people view transit as both environmental and racial justice.</li><li>Expanding public transit offers visible, short-term wins for climate credibility.</li><li>Local transit systems are often excluded from national climate conversations.</li><li>Framing transportation as a youth right reframes transit funding as essential, not optional.</li></ul>
<h3>Recommendations</h3>
<h3>Youth Transit Planning Boards</h3>
<ul><li>Require federal transit grants to include youth consultation and community mapping.</li></ul>
<h3>Clean Transit Investment</h3>
<ul><li>Prioritize electric buses, light rail, and bike infrastructure in climate resilience funds.</li></ul>
<h3>Transit Equity Zones</h3>
<ul><li>Establish low-fare/free zones in under-resourced neighborhoods with high youth density.</li></ul>
<h3>Mobility Literacy Campaigns</h3>
<ul><li>Launch peer-to-peer campaigns on safe, climate-conscious mobility for youth.</li></ul>
<h3>Conclusion</h3>
<p>Transit is not just about movement—it’s about opportunity, justice, and climate survival. By building a mobility agenda led by and for youth, the DNC can create sustainable cities, reduce emissions, and win trust</p>
