<h3>Executive Summary</h3>
<p>Millions of young Americans in vocational training programs lack formal civic education, despite being eligible voters. This memo proposes federal standards to integrate civic literacy into all career and technical education (CTE) pathways, ensuring that every young American understands their political rights and responsibilities.</p>
<h3>Background</h3>
<ul><li>Over 11 million students are enrolled in CTE or trade school programs.</li><li>Civic education mandates focus heavily on high schools and colleges, bypassing vocational institutions.</li><li>CTE students are more likely to be first-generation Americans, working-class youth, and students of color—groups often disenfranchised.</li></ul>
<h3>Analysis</h3>
<ul><li>Including civic education in trade schools closes a major democratic access gap.</li><li>Politically aware vocational students can become leaders in unionization, local politics, and issue-based activism.</li><li>This policy can help counter right-wing disinformation campaigns targeting non-college youth.</li></ul>
<h3>Recommendations</h3>
<h3>Curriculum Development</h3>
<ul><li>Create a federal Civic Literacy for Trades grant to fund curriculum development in vocational institutions.</li><li>Include content on voting, worker rights, government structure, and media literacy.</li></ul>
<h3>Educator Training</h3>
<ul><li>Partner with unions and civics educators to train trade school instructors in civic pedagogy.</li><li>Offer professional development credits for civic education workshops.</li></ul>
<h3>Community Engagement</h3>
<ul><li>Facilitate partnerships between trade schools and local election boards to host registration drives and civic forums.</li><li>Fund student-led civic action projects that connect political learning with community service.</li></ul>
<h3>Conclusion</h3>
<p>Mandating civic education in trade schools will ensure that all young Americans, regardless of educational path, are prepared to participate meaningfully in democracy. It is an essential step toward a truly inclusive political system.</p>
