<h3>Executive Summary</h3>
<p>Older women are frequently marginalized in post-conflict and disaster recovery policies, despite their roles as caregivers, community leaders, and survivors. This memo urges UN Women to prioritize older women’s rights in gender-based violence prevention, housing access, and post-disaster planning frameworks.</p>
<h3>Background</h3>
<ul><li>Older women face heightened risks of sexual and gender-based violence (SGBV) during and after crises.</li><li>They are often excluded from shelter design, land redistribution, and transitional justice processes.</li><li>Lifelong economic inequality results in severe post-crisis poverty for older women.</li><li>UN agencies lack data disaggregated by age and gender in most crisis settings.</li></ul>
<h3>Analysis</h3>
<p>Recovery policies that ignore older women:</p>
<ul><li><strong>Reinforce Gendered Poverty:</strong> Pensions and aid schemes rarely cover informal caregiving careers.</li><li><strong>Silence SGBV Survivors:</strong> Older women are not seen as “typical” victims, leading to invisibility in response systems.</li><li><strong>Exclude Leadership:</strong> Elders are often excluded from women's councils or youth-dominated advocacy platforms.</li><li><strong>Miss Intergenerational Impact:</strong> Ignoring older women weakens family and community recovery networks.</li></ul>
<h3>Recommendations</h3>
<h3>Inclusive Data and Policy Design</h3>
<ul><li>Mandate intersectional age/gender disaggregation in all recovery assessments.</li><li>Fund elder-led women’s organizations and post-crisis leadership programs.</li></ul>
<h3>Legal and Housing Access</h3>
<ul><li>Prioritize older women in post-conflict land, housing, and inheritance rights.</li><li>Include older SGBV survivors in reparations frameworks.</li></ul>
<h3>Anti-Ageism in Recovery</h3>
<ul><li>Train crisis workers and peacebuilders on ageism and elder gender rights.</li><li>Expand psychosocial support for older survivors of trauma.</li></ul>
<h3>Conclusion</h3>
<p>UN Women must expand its gender justice mandate to explicitly include older women in post-crisis response. Their voices are vital to rebuilding communities with care, equity, and memory.</p>
