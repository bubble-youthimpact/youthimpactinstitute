<h3>Executive Summary</h3>
<p>Older adults face rising economic precarity due to ageism in hiring, retention, and promotion. This memo calls on the ILO to strengthen enforcement mechanisms for age discrimination protections and promote flexible, equitable late-life employment across global labor markets.</p>
<h3>Background</h3>
<ul><li>The global workforce aged 55+ is projected to exceed 1 billion by 2050.</li><li>Many older adults are forced into early retirement or denied promotions.</li><li>Labor protections for age-based discrimination remain weak or poorly enforced.</li><li>Longer life expectancies demand rethinking retirement age and economic productivity models.</li></ul>
<h3>Analysis</h3>
<p>Age discrimination undermines older workers’ rights and economic security:</p>
<ul><li><strong>Hiring Bias:</strong> Many employers assume older workers lack adaptability or tech skills.</li><li><strong>Retention Gaps:</strong> Workplace accommodations are rarely provided for aging employees.</li><li><strong>Intersectional Discrimination:</strong> Older women, migrants, and disabled workers face compounded exclusion.</li><li><strong>Legal Inconsistencies:</strong> Definitions of “older workers” vary, and protections are often unenforced.</li></ul>
<h3>Recommendations</h3>
<h3>Strengthen Legal Protections</h3>
<ul><li>Standardize international definitions of age discrimination and enforcement standards.</li><li>Require public reporting on age diversity in corporate hiring and promotion.</li></ul>
<h3>Flexible Work Models</h3>
<ul><li>Promote part-time and hybrid work options for older employees.</li><li>Fund “career reentry” fellowships for senior workers.</li></ul>
<h3>Employer Accountability</h3>
<ul><li>Certify “age-inclusive” employers through ILO partnerships.</li><li>Provide tax incentives for hiring and training older workers.</li></ul>
<h3>Conclusion</h3>
<p>An age-inclusive labor force is essential to both economic sustainability and human rights. The ILO must lead in dismantling age discrimination and enabling dignified, flexible late-life employment.</p>
