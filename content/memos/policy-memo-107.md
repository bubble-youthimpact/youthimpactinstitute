<p>b]Executive Summary<br>Young conservatives are no longer ignoring climate—they’re redefining it. Frustrated by partisan paralysis, they’re leading new energy initiatives in red states: from decentralized solar to ag-tech efficiency. This memo outlines how the RNC can support a pragmatic, market-based, youth-driven climate innovation agenda that emphasizes energy sovereignty, rural prosperity, and future-focused jobs. Framing climate through independence, innovation, and responsibility can win over young voters without compromising core conservative values.</p>
<h3>Background</h3>
<ul><li>58% of young Republicans support expanding renewables alongside fossil fuels.</li><li>Red states, such as Texas and Iowa, lead in wind and solar production.</li><li>Youth innovators in conservative areas are founding clean energy startups, climate-focused ag-tech companies, and efficiency retrofitting businesses.</li><li>Energy inflation has fueled demand for localized, reliable power sources.</li><li>Distrust in federal climate mandates remains high—but appetite for energy innovation is growing.</li></ul>
<h3>Analysis</h3>
<ul><li>Young conservatives see energy innovation as patriotism, not partisanship.</li><li>Empowering youth-led clean tech aligns with rural job creation and energy security.</li><li>Ignoring this trend risks losing young independents and first-time Republican voters.</li><li>Framing climate action through economic freedom and resilience builds cross-generational bridges.</li></ul>
<h3>Recommendations</h3>
<h3>Red State Climate Startups Grant Fund</h3>
<ul><li>Offer seed funding and tax incentives for youth-founded energy startups in Republican districts.</li></ul>
<h3>Rural Solar and Storage Co-ops</h3>
<ul><li>Support young farmers and landowners in forming energy cooperatives using local resources.</li></ul>
<h3>Ag-Tech Innovation Accelerators</h3>
<ul><li>Partner with 4-H, FFA, and conservative colleges to incubate climate-smart farming solutions.</li></ul>
<h3>Youth Energy Apprenticeships</h3>
<ul><li>Create pathways for young tradespeople to enter high-growth energy resilience sectors, such as retrofitting and microgrid installation.</li></ul>
<h3>Conclusion</h3>
<p>The future of conservative climate leadership lies in local power, local people, and youth vision. By investing in energy independence through innovation, the RNC can lead—not follow—the next chapter of climate resilience. The young right is ready. It’s time to meet them there.</p>
