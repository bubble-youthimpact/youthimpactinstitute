<h3>Executive Summary</h3>
<p>AI content moderation often removes youth voices from digital platforms under vague or biased criteria. Marginalized youth—especially LGBTQ+ and political activists—are disproportionately silenced. UNESCO must ensure that AI governance protects youth expression and digital participation.</p>
<h3>Background</h3>
<p>AI moderation tools are used to detect hate speech, misinformation, and inappropriate content. While critical for safety, these systems also flag legitimate political expression, sexual health education, and minority language use. LGBTQ+ youth have reported their content being banned for “violating guidelines” while racist or harmful content remains online.</p>
<h3>Analysis</h3>
<p>Key concerns include:</p>
<p><strong>Censorship by Algorithm:</strong> Machine learning systems over-censor marginalized youth due to skewed training data.<br><strong>Lack of Appeals:</strong> Youth rarely understand or can challenge takedown decisions.<br><strong>Platform Discretion:</strong> Tech companies enforce vague terms with little transparency.<br><strong>Chilling Effect:</strong> Constant surveillance leads to self-censorship and silence.<br>Online expression is a pillar of youth civic engagement—especially in authoritarian or conservative contexts.</p>
<h3>Recommendations</h3>
<h3>Rights-Based AI Moderation</h3>
<ul><li>UNESCO should draft a youth-centered digital expression rights charter.</li><li>Require human oversight in content moderation affecting minors.</li></ul>
<h3>Transparency and Redress</h3>
<ul><li>Mandate clear explanations for takedowns and robust appeals processes.</li><li>Create youth digital rights ombudspersons in platform governance.</li></ul>
<h3>Platform Regulation</h3>
<ul><li>Advocate for regulation requiring transparency on moderation algorithms.</li><li>Promote user control over automated filters.</li></ul>
<h3>Youth Voice and Resilience</h3>
<ul><li>Fund digital literacy training on online expression rights.</li><li>Support youth-led digital platforms and alternative media ecosystems.</li></ul>
<h3>Conclusion</h3>
<p>Youth speech is not a glitch to be filtered out—it is the pulse of democracy. UNESCO must ensure AI moderation systems respect the voices of the next generation.</p>
