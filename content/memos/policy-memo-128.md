<h3>Executive Summary</h3>
<p>Weak enforcement of campaign finance laws enables dark money and illicit funding to influence elections unchecked. Increasing FEC funding and expertise, creating specialized units focused on digital and dark money investigations, and implementing expedited penalty processes will strengthen oversight. Collaboration with digital platforms and transparent reporting of enforcement actions are vital. Protecting whistleblowers and enhancing reporting channels further bolster compliance efforts.</p>
<h3>Background</h3>
<ul><li>The FEC has faced chronic underfunding and staffing shortages.</li><li>Digital political advertising and dark money complicate traditional enforcement.</li><li>Investigations are often slow, reducing deterrence.</li><li>Cooperation between regulators and social media platforms is inconsistent.</li><li>Transparency around enforcement outcomes is limited.</li><li>Whistleblowers face risks and lack protections.</li></ul>
<h3>Analysis</h3>
<p>Robust enforcement is essential to deter violations and maintain election integrity. Increased resources enable comprehensive investigations, particularly in digital and dark money arenas. Fast-tracking clear violations restores confidence and improves deterrence. Collaboration with platforms enhances oversight capacity. Public reporting improves accountability. Whistleblower protections encourage reporting of wrongdoing.</p>
<h3>Recommendations</h3>
<h3>Substantially Increase FEC Funding and Staffing</h3>
<ul><li>Secure budget increases to hire investigators, data scientists, and legal experts specializing in digital campaigns.</li></ul>
<h3>Create Specialized Enforcement Units</h3>
<ul><li>Establish Digital Ad Audit and Dark Money Investigation Task Forces.</li></ul>
<h3>Implement Expedited Investigation and Penalty Procedures</h3>
<ul><li>Develop fast-track resolution processes and scale penalties based on violation severity and recurrence.</li></ul>
<h3>Enhance Collaboration with Digital Platforms</h3>
<ul><li>Negotiate data-sharing agreements and encourage platform self-monitoring tools.</li></ul>
<h3>Increase Transparency of Enforcement Actions</h3>
<ul><li>Publish regular, detailed enforcement reports accessible to the public.</li></ul>
<h3>Promote Whistleblower Protections and Reporting Channels</h3>
<ul><li>Create confidential reporting systems with legal protections and incentives.</li></ul>
<h3>Conclusion</h3>
<p>Strengthening enforcement mechanisms is critical to closing loopholes exploited by dark money and illegal campaign funding. Enhanced resources, specialized expertise, collaboration, and transparency will restore public trust and safeguard democratic processes.</p>
