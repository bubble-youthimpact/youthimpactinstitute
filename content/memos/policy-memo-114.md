<h3>Executive Summary</h3>
<p>Mentorship is a critical component for successful youth entrepreneurship, providing guidance, skill development, and access to networks. Despite the clear benefits, no coordinated national mentorship infrastructure currently exists linking youth entrepreneurs to federal agencies and resources. This memo proposes the creation of a National Youth Entrepreneurship Mentorship Network (NYEMN) that connects young entrepreneurs with experienced mentors across sectors, facilitated and supported by federal agencies. Such a network would build youth capacity, increase startup survival rates, and democratize access to entrepreneurial knowledge.</p>
<h3>Background</h3>
<ul><li>Studies show mentorship increases business success rates by up to 70%, yet youth entrepreneurs often lack access to mentors.</li><li>Current mentorship efforts are fragmented and limited to private sector or local nonprofit programs without federal coordination.</li><li>Youth entrepreneurs face unique challenges, including balancing education, limited business knowledge, and network gaps.</li><li>Federal agencies possess resources and credibility but underutilize their potential to organize and promote mentorship.</li></ul>
<h3>Analysis</h3>
<p>A federally supported mentorship network can:</p>
<ul><li>Leverage public sector expertise and private sector mentors to provide tailored guidance to youth.</li><li>Foster cross-sector collaboration between business, academia, and government.</li><li>Address equity gaps by focusing on underrepresented youth through targeted recruitment of mentors.</li><li>However, without strategic coordination and digital infrastructure, such a network risks fragmentation or low engagement.</li></ul>
<h3>Recommendations</h3>
<p>The Office of Innovation and Entrepreneurship should:</p>
<ul><li>Launch a national digital platform for youth entrepreneurship mentorship with easy registration and matching tools.</li><li>Recruit mentors from federal agencies, private sector leaders, and successful youth entrepreneurs.</li><li>Provide mentor training focused on youth development and inclusive practices.</li><li>Collaborate with schools, incubators, and community groups to promote the network.</li><li>Implement data tracking and feedback systems to assess impact and refine the program.</li></ul>
<h3>Conclusion</h3>
<p>Creating a National Youth Entrepreneurship Mentorship Network offers a scalable, inclusive solution to youth capacity building in entrepreneurship. Federal leadership is essential to unite resources and stakeholders into a coherent ecosystem that nurtures youth innovators. This initiative will strengthen economic opportunity, innovation, and social mobility for the next generation.</p>
