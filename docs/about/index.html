Youth Impact Institute

Who We Are

Youth Impact Institute is a global, student-run think tank that connects high school students passionate about driving change through research, advocacy, and leadership. More than just a platform for discussion, YII fosters a community where young minds collaborate, explore complex global issues, and transform ideas into action. By bringing together future leaders from around the world, we create a movement that amplifies youth voices, encourages critical thinking, and empowers students to make a meaningful impact on society.

Our Mission & Vision

At Youth Impact Institute, we empower young minds to engage in research, debate, and advocacy, equipping them with the skills to analyze global challenges and drive real-world change. By fostering critical thinking, leadership, and collaboration with experts and policymakers, we ensure that youth voices actively shape a better future. We envision a world where young leaders play a central role in driving social transformation, pushing for meaningful reforms, and inspiring action beyond borders. Through research, advocacy, and leadership development, we cultivate a generation that is not only informed and engaged but also empowered to make a lasting impact on society.

Our Story

The Youth Impact Institute was born out of a simple yet powerful realization: the research and discussions we engaged in through school and extracurricular activities were not enough. We needed a space where we could take our learning beyond the classroom, delve deeper into global issues, and connect with students who shared our passion for meaningful change. More than just academic exercises, we wanted our research to lead to real-world impact—to transcend traditional learning and turn ideas into action.

What began as a small initiative soon evolved into a global student-run think tank, where high school students come together to explore pressing social, political, and economic challenges. Through in-depth research, rigorous debates, and collaborative discussions, we work to develop solutions that are not only intellectually sound but also actionable. Our platform provides students with opportunities to engage in advocacy, launch awareness campaigns, and even meet with global leaders to propose policy changes.

At our core, we believe that young people are not just the leaders of tomorrow but the changemakers of today. The Youth Impact Institute empowers students to think critically, lead boldly, and take concrete steps toward building a better world. By providing a space for intellectual growth and real-world impact, we are shaping a generation that doesn't just talk about change but actively works to create it.

What We Do

At Youth Impact Institute (YII), we focus on creating opportunities for students to engage in impactful activities that bridge research and real-world action. Our core activities include:

Research & Publications

  • Conduct in-depth research on critical global issues.
  • Write and publish policy memos, research papers, and articles.
  • Share student-led research with a global audience through publications.
  • Award quarterly prizes to recognize outstanding research contributions.

Advocacy & Campaigns

  • Launch digital and offline advocacy campaigns to address key social, political, and economic challenges.
  • Organize picketing campaigns and community outreach to raise awareness and drive change.
  • Host policy meetings and engage with stakeholders to push for meaningful reform.
  • Draft and present policy notes to influence public policy and decision-makers.

Implementation & Networking

  • Execute strategic initiatives to bring research and advocacy into real-world impact.
  • Organize exclusive conferences, debates, and workshops to develop leadership and advocacy skills.
  • Provide opportunities for networking with experts, organizations, and global leaders.
  • Foster collaboration through events like the UN Global Compact Leaders Summit to build valuable global connections.
