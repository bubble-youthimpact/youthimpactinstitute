UN Policy Memos

Policy Memo 12

Subject
Enhancing Youth Participation in Peacebuilding and Conflict Resolution
Date
February 03, 2024

Executive Summary

Youth are often disproportionately affected by conflict yet underrepresented in peacebuilding efforts. Their inclusion is critical to sustainable peace and social reconstruction. This memo proposes UNESCO champion youth participation in peace processes by supporting education on conflict resolution, creating platforms for dialogue, and fostering partnerships that empower young people as active peacebuilders.

Background

Youth involvement in peacebuilding contributes fresh perspectives and long-term stability.
However:

  • Youth participation in formal peace negotiations remains minimal.
  • Lack of education and resources limits young people’s capacity to engage constructively.
  • Marginalized youth, including refugees and displaced persons, face additional barriers.

Analysis

Key obstacles include:

  • Insufficient Peace Education: Curricula rarely incorporate conflict resolution and mediation skills.
  • Structural Exclusion: Youth are often sidelined in political and community decision-making.
  • Resource Scarcity: Limited funding for youth-led peace initiatives.
  • Safety Concerns: In conflict zones, youth face risks that hinder participation.

Recommendations

UNESCO should:

Peace Education Integration

  • Support the development of peacebuilding modules in formal and informal education systems.
  • Encourage experiential learning through role-play, dialogue, and community projects.

Youth Platforms and Networks

  • Establish forums and digital platforms for youth dialogue across conflict divides.
  • Promote youth-led peacebuilding initiatives with funding and mentorship.

Partnerships and Inclusion

  • Collaborate with local NGOs, governments, and international agencies to include youth voices in peace processes.
  • Address barriers faced by marginalized groups to ensure inclusive participation.

Safety and Support Measures

  • Develop protocols to protect youth participants in conflict-affected areas.
  • Provide psychosocial support to youth involved in peacebuilding activities.

Conclusion

Empowering youth as peacebuilders fosters durable peace and reconciliation. UNESCO’s leadership in promoting inclusive youth participation can transform conflict dynamics and support resilient communities worldwide.
