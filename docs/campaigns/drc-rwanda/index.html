Introduction

The ongoing conflict between the Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC) and Rwanda has reached an alarming stage in 2025, leaving millions of civilians displaced, communities devastated, and entire regions facing an unprecedented humanitarian catastrophe. The fighting has intensified, leading to the fall of major cities such as Goma and Bukavu, causing large-scale displacement and severe shortages of food, water, and medical aid. As the conflict rages on, civilians—regardless of nationality, ethnicity, or political affiliation—are bearing the brunt of the violence.

This campaign stands as a politically neutral initiative that does not take sides in the conflict. Instead, we call for urgent humanitarian intervention to protect the innocent and ensure that life-saving aid reaches those in desperate need. Our focus is solely on alleviating human suffering and advocating for immediate assistance to those affected by the crisis.

Objectives

  1. Establish Humanitarian Corridors: We demand the immediate establishment of humanitarian corridors to allow safe passage for civilians and aid workers, ensuring access to food, water, and medical assistance.
  2. Increase International Humanitarian Aid: We call on the United Nations, international organizations, and donor countries to urgently scale up aid efforts to address the growing crisis in eastern DRC and border areas.
  3. Ensure Protection of Civilians: We urge all parties involved in the conflict to uphold international humanitarian law, protect civilian populations, and prevent further displacement, violence, and human rights abuses.
  4. Hold Perpetrators of Humanitarian Violations Accountable: We call for an independent investigation into war crimes, including attacks on civilians, sexual violence, and forced displacement, to ensure accountability and justice for victims.
  5. Promote Regional and International Diplomatic Efforts: While this campaign remains politically neutral, we support diplomatic efforts that prioritize the protection and well-being of civilians and call for an immediate ceasefire to prevent further humanitarian catastrophe.

Why This Campaign is Important

The humanitarian crisis in the DRC and Rwanda is worsening by the day. According to reports from the United Nations and humanitarian organizations, more than 5.5 million people are internally displaced, while over one million have fled to neighboring countries. Mass displacement has led to overcrowded refugee camps, malnutrition, and a breakdown in essential healthcare services. The lack of food, clean water, and medical care puts millions of lives at risk, especially among women, children, and the elderly.

Hospitals and aid organizations are overwhelmed. In Goma and Bukavu, healthcare facilities struggle to treat the wounded while running out of essential medical supplies. Reports of sexual violence, child soldier recruitment, and attacks on civilians further highlight the urgent need for international intervention. Despite numerous calls for peace, violence persists, making it even more critical for humanitarian organizations to operate without obstruction.

How You Can Help

  • Sign the Petition: Support this campaign by signing our petition calling on international agencies and governments to act immediately.
  • Donate to Humanitarian Organizations: Contributions to relief organizations such as the Red Cross, Doctors Without Borders, and the World Food Programme can help deliver life-saving aid.
  • Spread Awareness: Share this campaign with your networks to amplify the call for action. Use social media, community discussions, and public forums to advocate for humanitarian support.
  • Pressure Policy Makers: Reach out to local and international leaders, urging them to take urgent steps to address the humanitarian crisis and support affected populations.

Call for Unity and Compassion

This campaign is a joint initiative that brings together both Rwandan and Congolese voices in a unified effort to protect human life. Our objective is not to assign blame or take a political stance but to ensure that the suffering of innocent people is not ignored. The conflict has already claimed too many lives, and the world must not look away.

We call upon all who believe in human dignity, compassion, and justice to stand with us in demanding urgent humanitarian action. Together, we can bring relief to those in dire need and work towards a future where peace and humanity prevail over violence and division.
